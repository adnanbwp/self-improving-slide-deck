## Beats

### Beat 1 — Comfort zone
- Purpose: Show the IM their squad as it is — and that their identity *is* delivery ownership, measured in velocity, flow, predictability, and sustainable pace. So when the metric breaks, it's personal.
- Deck prompts: What's the "before"? What did you always own and how did you prove it? What quiet gap is opening? (Slides: sharpened 441%/paradox hook → locate-yourself.)
- Style reminder: Lead with the number they can check; the first crack (output up, delivery flat) is the anomaly that pulls them in.

### Beat 2 — Trigger
- Purpose: Name what knocks them down — agents that act autonomously (coordination), *and* agents that complete the middle of the work faster than humans, decoupling the old unit of work from value (measurement).
- Deck prompts: What changed? Is it bad luck or not paying attention? Where do humans still dominate and where don't they? (Slides: **loop with agent=acts folded in (per 2026-07-14 addendum)** → **NEW strong-middle shape**.)
- Style reminder: Teach the strong-middle shape as a clean three — humans **Frame** (what/why), agents **Collapse** (construction), humans **Judge** (what's good). One diagram, no second mechanism slide on the main line.

### Beat 3 — Crisis, treasure in the dark
- Purpose: Peak the hole and find the treasure. The hole: velocity is now a vanity metric (an agent inflates points at near-zero cost), and it was *already* broken before agents — construction was never the constraint; human judgment/review is. The treasure: the honest new unit of squad success — flow of judged, durable outcomes through the judgment constraint — is a **delivery-measurement job that is yours**, and it sits exactly at the generate/decide boundary. You own the delivery outcome → the delivery outcome now flows through the boundary → therefore you govern the boundary.
- Deck prompts: What is learned/found here? Why you and not the tech lead? (Slides: first-person trust beat → pivot question → **NEW velocity-decoupled/broken-metric** → compressed upside+conditional evidence → generating-vs-governing → the boundary → **NEW the new unit of squad success (the A2 entailment)**.)
- Style reminder: This is the A2 kill. The entailment ("you own delivery, therefore you govern the boundary") replaces the weak land-grab argument. Pre-concede that velocity was always broken — that's the Data Sceptic trust move, not a concession.

### Beat 4 — Recovery, the climb
- Purpose: Hand them the model, the mechanism, *and the new instruments*. Own the boundary (centaur, HITL, capabilities), force the choice (the fork), then rebuild what you measure and how the cadences run.
- Deck prompts: What changes? What do you measure now? What happens to the ceremonies? (Slides: centaur → HITL patterns → the fork = the crisis choice → three capabilities → **NEW proposed frameworks: attention budget + token budget (two slides per Rex S3 — attention budget primary, token budget subordinate)** → **REFRAMED VERDICT/cadence-rebuild (slide 19)** → failure modes.)
- Style reminder: The fork stays the emotional pivot — evolve or dissolve. Present the two frameworks explicitly **as proposals** (original territory; no published framework exists) — that honesty is the leading-voice move and a trust beat, not a hedge.

### Beat 5 — Better place
- Purpose: The new normal — the role evolved, delivery owned *through* the boundary, measurement rebuilt, governance moving with the engineers. One action that starts it this week.
- Deck prompts: What's the new normal? Harder to knock down next time? (Slides: squad audit + measurement question → 12-month path → CTA = audit + baseline.)
- Style reminder: End on the lever they hold — run the audit **and** take one honest baseline this sprint. Protect the payoff line.

## Slide-19 reframe (resolve the self-contradiction)

Current line — *"Governance isn't a new ceremony. It's the old ones, upgraded."* — directly contradicts Adnan's "can't slap an AI sticker on old practices" and his "cadences must fundamentally change or be replaced."

Research resolution (synthesis contradiction-map #3; Historian H4): the *coordination function* of a ceremony survives, by **shrinking and decoupling** into a judgment ritual (trunk-based dev, feature flags, async standups are the precedent) — but the **content and metrics inside it must be rebuilt** around agent evidence (logs, eval results, attention budget). Neither an AI sticker nor a bonfire.

**Reframed headline:**
> **"The ceremony survives as a judgment ritual. The metric inside it does not."**

**Supporting line:**
> "This is the opposite of an AI sticker. Shrink each ceremony to the human-judgment call it now exists for — then rebuild what it measures: velocity and story points *out*; flow of judged, durable outcomes, attention budget, and agent cost *in*."

VERDICT stays underneath as the pillar→ritual scaffold, but its framing flips from "upgrade the old ceremony" to "which judgment each shrunken ritual now protects, and which number replaces the old one." That preserves VERDICT's Rule-of-three value while removing the contradiction, and it turns slide 19 into the on-ramp for the reframed sprint review / Daily Scrum where the new instruments (from slide D1/D2) actually get read.
